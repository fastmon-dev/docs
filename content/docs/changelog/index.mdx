The latest from fastmon, across the dashboard, API, platform, and tracker. You can filter the feed by area.

We cover the changes you should know about, not every internal tweak. The exception is the **tracker** (the script you embed on your site): we document it in full, because it runs in your visitors' browsers, where even a small change can affect what your pages measure.

<Changelog>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-30" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Shareable metric-panel view">

The **Over time / Distribution / Both** toggle on the metric detail pages (LCP, FCP, CLS, INP, TTFB, Page Weight, …) is now reflected in the URL, with a **share-link button** — a copied link re-opens the exact view, time range pinned. Shared links to a panel view or a breakdown sub-panel now reliably keep their view (the `view`, `pview`, and `panel` params survive navigation) and scroll all the way to the target panel.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-30" type="changed" area="dashboard" title="Attribution tables show every row, with per-column aggregation">

The slowest/worst pages, the breakdown grids, and the Cache and Errors attribution tables no longer hide low-volume rows behind a **≥1%-of-pageviews (min. 30)** floor — every row is shown; narrow by volume yourself in the Explorer (for example `beacons:ge:N`). And a count-only metric (Requests / Visitors) next to a value metric no longer forces the whole table to **Count** — each column uses its own aggregation, so **LCP shows its p75** instead of a meaningless count (the Explorer KPI tiles too).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-30" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Explorer: distribution of a dimension over time">

In the Explorer you can now group by a categorical dimension — for example CDN or origin cache status — and chart how each of its values (HIT, MISS, EXPIRED, …) develops **over time** with a Line, Stacked Line, or Stacked Bar view, instead of only the aggregate breakdown. Stacked views toggle between **100%** (each value's share per interval) and **absolute** counts. You can overlay a second metric, such as TTFB, as a dashed line on a right-hand axis for context. Choosing a Group-by dimension no longer forces the chart to Bar — Line, Stacked, and Pie are kept where they make sense — and the chart-type picker greys out options that can't render the current query.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-30" type="fixed" area="dashboard" title="Percentage metrics in the Explorer">

Ratio metrics such as cache hit rate now render as a real percentage (for example **80%**) in the Explorer charts and tables, instead of showing “0.8%”.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-27" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Good / Needs-improvement / Poor split on the distribution cards">

The dashboard's distribution metric cards now show the **Good / Needs-improvement / Poor split** as colour-coded percentages in the card header — for every metric with thresholds, including FCP, TTFB, and Page Load, not just the Core Web Vitals — while the rating badge and trend arrow stay in place. The card histogram is larger, and its bars are now both hoverable for detail and clickable to open the metric detail page.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-27" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Filter, exclude, and deep-link breakdown panels">

The “What's driving it” breakdown rows now carry hover **+ / −** actions to filter to or exclude a value in place, matching the per-row grid panels; exclude is shown disabled with a reason for dimensions that can't be excluded, such as page URLs. Each breakdown panel also has a share button that deep-links straight to that panel — opening the link scrolls to it and briefly highlights it.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-27" type="changed" area="dashboard" title="Cleaner distribution presentation">

Detail pages no longer repeat the value distribution at the bottom of the page — it now lives only in the top “Over time” panel. Breakdown grid panels show each value's share of pageviews next to the absolute count, and share links restore the chosen chart view (Over time, Distribution, or both) of an expanded row, not just the row itself. CDN and origin cache breakdowns now include an “unknown” bucket for responses with no cache header, but only when at least 1% of views have a known cache status, so an all-uncached site no longer shows a useless “unknown 100%” panel. On the Errors page, the top-right “Open in Explorer” button has been removed — record-level drill-down is reached from the tables instead.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-26" type="changed" area="api" title="Ad-blocked tracker count is now returned by the API">

The number of tracker calls dropped as ad-blocked — already excluded from your error rate, but not reported anywhere — is now returned by the read APIs. The fetch/XHR endpoint breakdown and the per-beacon detail include a `blocked` count alongside `aborts`, and the error-types overview returns a new `blocked_count`. Your error rate is unchanged; this only surfaces how many calls were excluded, so the dashboard can show “N excluded as ad-blocked” instead of silently dropping them. See the [API reference](/en/api).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-26" type="changed" area="dashboard" title="Metric detail pages redesigned around one “Over time” panel">

Each metric detail page (LCP, INP, CLS, TBT, Page Weight, and the load-timing pages) now leads with a single **“Over time” panel** that you switch between the percentile trend, the value distribution, or both side by side. The distribution buckets and the Good/Poor zones are clickable: they open the Explorer scoped to that value range, carrying over your current time range. Short windows (2 hours or less) now chart by the minute instead of by the hour, so a one-hour view is no longer nearly empty.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-26" type="new" area="dashboard" title="“What’s driving it” breakdown on the detail pages">

Every metric detail page now has a breakdown table you can switch across dimensions (Page, Country, Device, Browser with or without version, Connection, and CDN/Origin cache), with a TTFB column for context and a Most viewed / Worst / Best toggle. Expanding a row reveals the same over-time panel scoped to that value, plus a grid of the other dimensions where you can filter to or exclude a value in place, or open it in the Explorer. This expandable view is the same on the metric detail pages and in the Explorer: a grouped Explorer result row opens the exact same panel and grid.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-26" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Desktop / Mobile quick filter">

An **All / Desktop / Mobile** quick filter now sits next to the time picker on the metric detail pages, the Dashboard, and Analytics, applying the same device filter as the filter bar. The toolbar (percentile · time range · device · filters · refresh) is now identical across these views.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-26" type="changed" area="dashboard" title="Clearer Explorer filters, breadcrumb drill-down, and pinned share links">

The Explorer’s two filter rows are now labelled **Query filters** (applied on the server) and **Result filters** (applied in your browser), on a single line. The Good/Needs-improvement/Poor status colour is always on for value metrics, and count metrics (such as requests by URL) now expand to the breakdown grid and a trend instead of an empty distribution. Drilling in from a metric extends the breadcrumb with where you came from (for example “Core Web Vitals › LCP › Explorer”), and a back arrow returns you to the exact spot, including the row you had expanded. Shareable links now pin the current time range to absolute timestamps, so a shared link always opens the same window. The standalone **Pages** view has been retired in favour of **Analytics**, which now sits higher in the sidebar.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-26" type="fixed" area="dashboard" title="Large data volumes and mobile layout">

Transfer-size and page-weight values now scale up to **GB and TB** instead of capping at MB (a ~1 TB total no longer reads as “1069213.0 MB”), and large third-party call counts show in compact form (for example “1.1M”) with the full number on hover. On small screens, the expanded breakdown grid no longer overflows off-screen, its row actions are always visible on touch, and the time picker no longer runs off the left edge.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-26" type="changed" area="dashboard" title="Error rate no longer counts ad-blocked trackers or page-teardown noise">

Third-party trackers blocked by an ad-blocker or tracking protection (Bing UET, Meta Pixel, Google Tag Manager, and similar) no longer count as errors, and neither do requests the browser cancels when a visitor leaves the page. They are tagged and kept, not dropped, so your error rate reflects real, fixable problems. One real dashboard fell from a ~60% error rate that was driven almost entirely by a blocked `bat.bing.com`.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-26" type="changed" area="tracker" title="Tracker stops reporting page-teardown failures as errors">

When a visitor navigates away or closes the tab, the browser cancels in-flight requests and can interrupt running scripts. The tracker now detects teardown (via `pagehide` and `visibilitychange`) and counts those network failures as aborts rather than errors, and drops the JavaScript exceptions thrown during teardown, so neither inflates your error count.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-25" type="new" area="platform" title="Passwordless login with passkeys">

Sign in with a device passkey in one step, with no password and no separate 2FA code. Manage passkeys under Account → Security (add, name, rename, remove). Sensitive actions (regenerating your API key, toggling 2FA, changing your email, connecting an app, deleting your account) now ask for fresh re-authentication ("step-up") with your strongest enrolled factor, a passkey or TOTP.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-25" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Cache-hit visibility">

New CDN Cache and Origin Cache (`HIT`/`MISS`) columns and a cache-status filter in the beacon explorer.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-25" type="fixed" area="platform" title="AI “Ask” error drill-down">

The AI Ask error drill-down returns its summary and time-series correctly instead of erroring on every call.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-24" type="new" area="platform" title="Cross-domain trackers">

Run a single tracker across several domains of the same codebase (for example `shop.de` and `shop.fr`); beacons are routed to the correct per-domain site automatically, so per-site analytics and access control are preserved. Create, edit, rotate hashes, and delete them on the organization's Sites page, with a "detected domains" list that suggests unregistered hosts to add as sites.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-24" type="new" area="platform" title="Partner self-service">

Organizations flagged as partners can provision and manage client organizations: create a client (optionally emailing an owner invite), list managed clients, view aggregated beacon usage, and detach a client. Organization → Team also lists partners that can manage your org and lets owners revoke that access.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-24" type="changed" area="dashboard" title="Collector modes">

The binary fastmon / first-party collector toggle is now three modes, each shown with a first-party / third-party badge: Default (fastmon's collector), Custom (a URL you control), and Relative (a same-origin `/c/<hash>` endpoint). (API: a new `collector_mode` field on sites and trackers.)

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-24" type="fixed" area="platform" title="Deleting a site no longer fails on child rows">

`DELETE /sites/{id}` no longer errors when the site has releases, weekly summaries, or feedback.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-23" type="fixed" area="platform" title="IDN and trailing-dot domains no longer lose traffic">

Internationalized hostnames (e.g. `münchen.de`) and trailing-dot FQDNs were rejected or dropped at ingest; hostnames are now canonicalized consistently at registration and ingest.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-23" type="fixed" area="dashboard" title="Per-domain breakdown on multi-domain setups">

Beacons now record the host the tracker actually reported instead of the configured site domain, so the per-domain breakdown is correct again. Historical rows keep their old value.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-23" type="fixed" area="dashboard" title="Resource-load errors group separately">

In anonymous mode, distinct broken first-party resources on the same host no longer collapse into a single error (applies to new beacons).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-22" type="new" area="api" title="More beacon fields and columns">

New `browser_version` field on the visitors list, and five new projectable columns on `/analytics/beacons` (`version`, `tracker_version`, `site_id`, `lifecycle`, `delivery_method`). See the [API reference](/en/api).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-22" type="changed" area="api" title="More accurate web-vitals percentiles">

Median and percentile values for web vitals in long-range analytics are computed more accurately, correcting a systematic underestimate of the median.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-21" type="changed" area="dashboard" title="Rebuilt time-range picker">

The picker now leads with an absolute range that also accepts relative input (`now-6h`, or shorthand like `4h` / `30d`); relative ranges stay live against "now", with a "Now" pin and an `r` keyboard shortcut to refresh. Metric thresholds can now be typed in natural units, e.g. `lcp >= 2.5s` or `total_byte_weight < 512kb`.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-21" type="fixed" area="dashboard" title="Visitors-list filter crash">

Filtering the visitors list by device type, browser, country, or OS no longer errors.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-20" type="changed" area="dashboard" title="“Sessions” is now “Visitors”, along an identity axis">

The `sessions` metric is replaced by `visitors` everywhere: charts, the metric picker, and alert rules. A visitor is counted by identity: `stitch` (edge-derived, consent-free, the default) or `session` (consent-gated). The explorer gained a Stitch/Session switch with a per-row identity badge, and each site has a new default-visitor-identity setting. See [Visitors and sessions](/en/concepts/sessions).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-20" type="new" area="platform" title="Connected apps (OAuth)">

fastmon can authorize first-party app integrations: an in-app consent screen, and a "Connected apps" tab in account settings where you can review and disconnect authorized apps.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-20" type="changed" area="platform" title="Brand lowercased to “fastmon”">

The brand is now written lowercase across the app, authentication, emails, and SEO metadata.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-20" type="fixed" area="platform" title="Invited users land in their organization">

Accepting an invite no longer drops you into a second empty organization, including Google/GitHub sign-up.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-17" type="changed" area="tracker" title="Full stack-frame paths kept for de-minification">

The tracker keeps the complete minified stack-frame path so JavaScript errors de-minify to the correct source location.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-17" type="fixed" area="tracker" title="Fetch/XHR calls flushed mid-pageview">

The tracker now reports every fetch/XHR call during a pageview, not just slow outliers, so the Fetch/XHR view is complete.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-17" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Value distributions and shareable deep links">

Core Web Vitals and performance/network detail pages show the metric's distribution with Good / Needs-improvement / Poor zones and p50/p75/p90 markers; each page row expands to its own distribution. Share buttons reopen the exact page or row, and the per-page error breakdown plus multi-value and exclude (`≠`) filters arrived alongside.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-16" type="new" area="tracker" title="Soft navigations marked as nav_type=softnav">

The tracker marks client-side route changes as `nav_type=softnav`, so single-page-app navigations are distinguished from full page loads in analytics.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-16" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Fetch/XHR (AJAX) monitoring">

A dedicated page under Network analyzes your app's fetch/XHR calls: p75/p95 tail latency, a blocking-call filter, aborts counted apart from errors, response size, a per-call backend-phase mini-waterfall, and a first-party / third-party scope toggle. Capture is controlled per site by a "Store subrequests" toggle. See [Analytics](/en/analytics).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-15" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Device-type breakdown, auto theme, richer beacon detail">

A device-type tab (desktop / mobile / tablet), an "Auto" theme that follows your OS preference, and a far richer per-beacon detail panel (server-timing phases, cache status, called endpoints, attribution).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-14" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Command palette (⌘K)">

One searchable menu drives the whole app: jump to any page, switch organization or site, change the time range and granularity, and add or clear filters. The filter control also moved to the header, next to the time picker, with an active-filter count badge.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-06-14" type="removed" area="dashboard" title="Per-site tag filter in the sidebar">

The sidebar tag filter only narrowed the site list and never affected analytics, so it was removed; tags are planned to return as a real cross-site analytics scope. See [Sites](/en/concepts/sites).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-05-28" type="changed" area="platform" title="API tokens are now “API keys”">

The personal access token is now called an **API key** consistently across the API, the SDK examples, and account settings. Same credential and behavior; only the name changed, to match the backend. See [Authentication](/en/api/authentication).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-05-27" type="fixed" area="dashboard" title="URL drill-down dropped rows on full-collection sites">

On sites in full collection mode, the per-URL drill-down silently dropped some rows; it now returns the complete breakdown.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-05-26" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Minute-level granularity">

Analytics queries over short windows can now bucket by the minute (in addition to hour/day), so very recent traffic and incidents show up at the right resolution.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-05-21" type="changed" area="platform" title="Raw IP, User-Agent, and referrer no longer reach the backend">

A privacy-focused edge cutover: the edge derives everything fastmon needs (country, device, referrer source) and the backend never receives the raw IP address, User-Agent string, or full referrer URL. Less personal data is stored, by design. See [Privacy](/en/privacy).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-05-21" type="new" area="platform" title="Stitch: a consent-free cross-pageview identifier">

A new edge-derived, bounded-lifetime identifier (`stitch`) links pageviews from the same visitor without cookies or consent. It later became the default visitor identity. See [Visitors and sessions](/en/concepts/sessions).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-05-21" type="new" area="dashboard" title="Campaign attribution (UTM + click IDs)">

Traffic now carries `utm_*` parameters and ad-platform click IDs, so you can break analytics down by campaign source, medium, and campaign (Plausible-aligned).

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-05-21" type="changed" area="dashboard" title="Referrer reclassified into source and medium">

Instead of a raw referrer URL, referrers are bucketed into a normalized **source** and **medium** (Snowplow-style), so the referrer breakdown is clean and groupable.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-05-21" type="removed" area="tracker" title="Five low-value fields retired from the beacon">

The tracker no longer collects `device_memory`, `hw_concurrency`, `save_data`, `prefers_dark`, or `prefers_reduced_motion`: a data-minimization pass that shrinks the payload and the data stored about each visitor.

</ChangelogEntry>

<ChangelogEntry date="2026-05-21" type="changed" area="tracker" title="Tracker slimmed down">

The tracker no longer reads `document.referrer`, `matchMedia`, or User-Agent buckets (the edge derives those now), making the embedded script smaller and the data it sends leaner.

</ChangelogEntry>

</Changelog>
