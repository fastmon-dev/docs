Fastmon's domain model is intentionally small. If you understand five
resources you understand the whole product.

  <Card title="Organizations" href="/en/concepts/organizations" description="Top-level account container. Owns sites, members, tokens." />
  <Card title="Sites" href="/en/concepts/sites" description="A monitored property. Has its own beacon hashes and settings." />
  <Card title="Sessions" href="/en/concepts/sessions" description="A short window of page-views from the same browser. Anonymous by construction." />
  <Card title="Releases" href="/en/concepts/releases" description="A versioned marker on a site. Used for before/after comparisons." />
  <Card title="Collection modes" href="/en/collection-modes" description="anonymous / consent / full: what the beacon does in the browser." />
</Cards>

1. [Organizations](/en/concepts/organizations): what an account

5. [Releases](/en/concepts/releases): for before/after deploys.
