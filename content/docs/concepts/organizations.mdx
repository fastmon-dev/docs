An **organization** is your company's or team's account in fastmon. It holds the plan, billing, and members. Every [site](/en/concepts/sites), invite, and notification rule belongs to exactly one organization.

## What's on an organization

| Field             | Type                                      | Notes                                                                 |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `id`              | UUID v7                                   | Stable identifier. Used in API URLs (`/organizations/{id}/…`). |
| `name`            | string                                    | Human-readable. Shows in the org switcher.                            |
| `plan`            | enum                                      | `none`, `free`, `one`, `partner`, or `enterprise`.                    |
| `members`         | list of `{ user_id, role }`               | Roles: `owner`, `member`, `viewer`. The payload also carries your own `role` and `permissions`. |

## Plans

| Plan          | When you'd be on it                                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `none`        | The starting state. Nobody has chosen anything yet.          |
| `free`        | fastmon One without payment: every feature, unlimited pageviews and applications. |
| `one`         | The one plan you can buy, sold as **fastmon One**. Everything is included. |
| `partner`     | Agencies. Manage **partner clients** (sub-organizations you operate on behalf of someone else). |
| `enterprise`  | **fastmon Enterprise**. Custom limits, custom retention, signed DPA. |

A new organization starts on `none` and leaves it by exactly three routes: an approval starts its trial, a checkout buys a plan, or a partner provisions it.

The plan gates features and quotas, never which metrics are collected. `features` on the organization lists each feature as `covered` by a partner, `granted` by fastmon, `included` in the plan, or `locked`.

**What a plan is measured in.** The billed unit is the [application](/en/concepts/applications), and only a **production** application takes a slot: a development application is free and unlimited. A plan includes a number of production slots and a monthly pageview volume; both can be extended with permanent add-ons bought in advance. Creating or promoting a production application past the included slots is refused with `402 app_limit_reached` (see [Errors](/en/api/errors)), and an organization with no entitlement at all gets `402 billing_required` or `402 trial_expired`.

**The pageview volume counts each stored pageview once**, bots included. A sampled pageview counts as stored, not scaled up, and the last 15 minutes count once they are finalized. [`GET /organizations/{id}/usage`](/en/api/organizations/getOrganizationUsage) answers it as `pageview_count` with its `time_range`.

**E-invoices.** Business customers in Germany, the Netherlands, and Belgium with a VAT id also get each invoice over Peppol. The VAT id is the Peppol identifier, so it has to match your registration.

## Roles

A role is a fixed bundle of **permissions**. A permission is one capability in `resource:action` form (`analytics:read`, `release:write`, `member:manage`); every API route names the one it needs, and the dashboard shows or hides actions by the same list. `GET /permissions` lists every permission with a description and the roles that hold it.

| Role     | Can do                                                                                       |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `owner`  | Everything a member can, plus: organization settings (name, AI and synthetic monitoring consent), members and their roles, notification rules, the audit log, paying (`billing:manage`), transfer, and deletion. |
| `member` | Everyday work: create and change sites, applications, releases, and synthetic pages; start synthetic runs; ask the AI assistant; invite people; read the invoices (`billing:read`); on a `partner` organization, manage its clients (`partner:manage`). |
| `viewer` | Read-only. Sees dashboards, analytics, releases, synthetic results, notification rules, and the member list. Changes nothing, triggers nothing that costs money or sends mail, and does not see billing. |

**Billing is two permissions, not one role.** `billing:read` opens the invoices, the billing address, and the VAT id; `billing:manage` moves the money.

The bundles nest: a member holds everything a viewer holds, an owner everything a member holds. [Personal API keys](/en/api/authentication) are cut from the same bundles: a key's scopes are permissions, intersected with your current role on every request. An [organization key](/en/api/authentication#two-kinds-of-key) belongs to the organization instead and stands on its scopes alone, capped at issue time. Managing organization keys and [connected apps](/en/api/oauth) takes `org_key:manage`, which members and owners hold and viewers do not.

An organization can have several owners. Owners change roles under **Members** (`PATCH /members/{member_id}`); only an owner can hand out the owner role. The last owner cannot be demoted or leave while other members remain (`last_owner`). **Transfer ownership** hands the organization to another member in one step.

### Partner access [#partner-access]

In a client organization, a partner employee acts with the role they hold in the partner organization: `owner`, `member`, or `viewer`. With two agencies serving one client, the stronger role applies. A direct membership in the client wins.

Partner access can issue organization keys and connect org-owned apps in a client, such as the Shopware plugin through OAuth. Even an agency owner cannot do the following in a client:

- Delete it, remove its last member, transfer it, or manage its billing.
- Demote or remove its owners, or grant the owner role while it has an owner.
- Add members without an invite, or manage its partner links.
- Issue keys for the client's own clients.

**Partner-minted credentials** outlive the partnership. The client's key and connection lists mark them with `via_partner_org_id`, and detaching a partner lists them.

The client rotates the keys and disconnects the apps. A rotation by a client member clears the mark.

The `partner.removed` audit entry lists them as `credentials_left`. It shows in the audit logs of both organizations.

## Common scenarios

### One product, one org

One organization, one billing relationship, several sites under it (`acme-marketing`, `acme-app`, `acme-docs`).

### Multiple unrelated products

For two unrelated brands with separate billing and access, create two organizations. You can be a member of both.

### Agency running client sites

Use the `partner` plan. Each client is a **partner client**: a sub-organization the agency runs. Agency users see all clients; the client sees only itself. A partner organization can also issue [one API key that follows its client list](/en/api/authentication#creating-a-key) instead of one key per client.

A partner covers features and pageviews per client with `PATCH /organizations/{org_id}/partner/clients/{client_org_id}`, within the contract at `GET /organizations/{org_id}/partner/contract`.

A **member** of the partner organization creates a client, hands it over with `owner_email`, and detaches it. A member of the client revokes the agency's access. Both need direct membership; see [Partner access](#partner-access).

The [Partnerfloor](/en/analytics#partnerfloor) shows the live traffic of every client.

## Common surprises

- **The org switcher is just UI.** API requests scope by the URL (`/organizations/{id}/…`), not by whichever tab you've got open.
- **Members are global fastmon users.** Inviting an existing user reuses their account.
- **An invite waits on the account.** Someone who signed up without the mail link finds their open invites on the start screen.
- **Adding a member directly needs an owner.** `POST /organizations/{id}/members` requires `member:manage`. Members invite instead, and an invite carries the role.
- **Sites can't move between organizations.** Ask support if you need this.
