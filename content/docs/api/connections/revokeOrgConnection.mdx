          Disconnect an app.


          Spends every refresh token under the grant at once, so the app cannot
          rotate

          its way back in and has to go through consent again. Access tokens
          already

          issued are not hunted down - they expire on their own within minutes,
          which

          is the price of not keeping a revocation list and the reason that
          lifetime

          is short.


          No step-up, like every other revoke here: revocation is the fail-safe

          direction, and a password prompt is a cost paid at exactly the wrong
          moment.

          Session-only, because ending the credentials an incident response
          depends on

          must not itself be doable with a credential.
